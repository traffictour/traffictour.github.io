GAME OVERVIEW & EXPLANATION

Welcome to Endless Truck, the ultimate test of endurance and skill behind the wheel of a monster truck! Prepare yourself for an adrenaline-fueled journey through rugged terrain as you race against the clock and collect coins along the way.

What We Offer

Endless Truck offers an exhilarating mix of high-speed racing and challenging obstacle courses. Choose from a variety of monster trucks and embark on an endless adventure across diverse landscapes, from deserts to snow-covered mountains.

Gameplay

In Endless Truck, your objective is to drive as far as possible while avoiding obstacles and collecting coins. Use the arrow keys or WASD to control your monster truck as it barrels through the terrain, and be prepared to perform jumps and stunts to overcome obstacles.

Controls:

  • Use the up arrow key or W to accelerate.
  • Use the down arrow key or S to brake/reverse.
  • Use the left and right arrow keys or A and D to tilt your truck forward and backward.

Features

  • Multiple monster trucks to choose from, each with unique abilities
  • Dynamic terrain and obstacles that pose different challenges
  • Upgrade your trucks with nitro boosts, better tires, and more
  • Compete against friends or other players on the global leaderboard
  • Unlock achievements and new trucks as you progress through the game

Get ready to put your driving skills to the test and conquer the endless terrain in Endless Truck!
