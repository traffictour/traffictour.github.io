ABOUT MR RACER CAR RACING

MR Racer Car Racing is a top-tier 3D racing game featuring over 100 challenging career missions, realistic 1st-person cockpit driving, and stunning visual graphics! Drive supercars through city streets, desert highways, and snowy mountain passes.

Experience realistic vehicle physics, functional dashboard meters, and high-octane traffic overtakes. Customize your dream ride with custom paints, rims, and engine upgrades!

Key Features

  • 100+ Career Levels: Conquer time trials, chase targets, and high-speed highway challenges.
  • 1st Person Cockpit View: Immersive dashboard camera with working speedometer and steering animations.
  • Exotic Supercars: Drive high-end luxury sports cars and hypercars.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Passing traffic closely at speeds above 100 km/h earns extra bonus cash and nitro refill energy!

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer & Drive: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys.
  • Nitrous Boost: Spacebar or Shift.
  • Change Camera Angle: C key.
  • Sound Horn: H key.

Pro Tips

  • Use 1st person cockpit view for tighter steering accuracy when threading through dense traffic!
