ABOUT KART RACE 3D

Kart Race 3D brings fast-paced, colorful go-kart tournament racing right to your browser! Drive nimble karts around winding tracks, trigger drift boosts on sharp bends, and use fun power-ups to out-speed rival drivers.

Customize your racer, unlock faster kart chassis, and compete across multiple championship cups to earn the title of 3D Kart Champion!

Key Features

  • Action Kart Racing: Drift around tight hairpin corners to earn mini-turbo boosts.
  • Fun Power-Ups: Pick up speed boosters, shields, and projectile items on the track.
  • Vibrant 3D Circuits: Race through tropical beaches, castle tracks, and city parks.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Hold the drift key into corners until blue sparks appear to trigger a mini-turbo boost out of the turn!

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer Kart: A, D or Left / Right Arrow.
  • Accelerate: W or Up Arrow.
  • Brake / Reverse: S or Down Arrow.
  • Drift / Hop: Shift key.
  • Use Item / Power-Up: Spacebar.

Pro Tips

  • Save your defensive shield power-up until opponents fire homing projectiles at you!
