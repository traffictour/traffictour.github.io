ABOUT HILL CLIMB PIXEL CAR

Hill Climb Pixel Car combines retro 2D pixel art graphics with challenging physics-based hill driving! Drive retro vehicles up steep mountain passes, bouncy hills, and rugged countryside terrain while collecting coins and gas canisters.

Manage your vehicle's pitch balance to avoid flipping over backwards on steep climbs while ensuring you don't run out of fuel before reaching the next checkpoint!

Key Features

  • Charming Pixel Art: Retro 8-bit visual aesthetic and energetic sound effects.
  • Physics Hill Driving: Balance throttle and tilt to conquer steep inclines.
  • Fuel & Coin Management: Collect gas cans and coins to upgrade engine power and tires.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Ease off the gas pedal when airborne to keep your vehicle level for a smooth hill landing.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Gas / Tilt Forward: D or Right Arrow.
  • Brake / Tilt Backward: A or Left Arrow.
  • Turbo Boost: Spacebar.

Pro Tips

  • Upgrade your vehicle's tires first to improve grip on slippery pixel hill slopes!
