ABOUT DRIFT ESCAPE

Drift Escape is a high-octane police pursuit game where your drift skills are your only way out! Out-maneuver aggressive police cruisers and tactical SWAT vehicles by sliding through narrow alleyways and tight city corners.

Tricking police cars into crashing into each other earns bonus rewards. Upgrade your getaway car's speed and armor to survive intense 5-star police chases!

Key Features

  • High-Stakes Police Pursuit: Dodge relentless police chases and roadblocks.
  • Tactical Drift Mechanics: Slide 180 and 360 degrees to fool pursuing cruisers.
  • Custom Getaway Cars: Unlock armored muscle cars and swift drift tuners.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Lead police cruisers toward obstacles like buildings and concrete walls, then drift sharply at the last second!

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer / Drive: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys.
  • Handbrake Drift: Spacebar.
  • Nitro Boost: Shift key.

Pro Tips

  • Drifting close to obstacles confuses AI pathfinding, causing police cars to crash into walls!
