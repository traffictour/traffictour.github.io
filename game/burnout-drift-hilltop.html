ABOUT BURNOUT DRIFT HILLTOP

Burnout Drift Hilltop is a dedicated drift simulator set on challenging mountain peak roads and winding hilltop switchbacks! Push tuned drift cars to the limit as you slide around cliffside hairpin turns.

Maintain continuous drift angles to build massive point multipliers, earn drift score bonuses, and tune your car's camber, gear ratios, and turbo boost.

Key Features

  • Hilltop Drift Tracks: Steep elevation changes and tight hairpin mountain corners.
  • Realistic Physics Engine: Control counter-steering, weight transfer, and wheel spin.
  • Deep Car Tuning: Customize suspension, tire pressure, and engine horsepower.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Feather the throttle during long drifts to prevent spinning out near dangerous cliff drops.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer & Accelerate: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys.
  • Handbrake Drift: Spacebar.
  • Change Camera: C key.

Pro Tips

  • Counter-steer sharply into the direction of the turn to hold a stable drift angle down the hill!
