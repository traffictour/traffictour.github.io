ABOUT 2 PLAYER DARK RACING

2 Player Dark Racing is an intense night-time racing game designed for thrilling head-to-head competition! Race through illuminated city streets and pitch-black highway circuits under the moonlight. Choose to compete against smart AI drivers or battle against a friend on the same device.

With sleek midnight supercars, neon accents, and high-velocity drift corners, 2 Player Dark Racing delivers maximum speed and competitive action. Upgrade your vehicle's engine, accelerate past rivals on dark turns, and cross the finish line first!

Key Features

  • 2-Player Mode: Challenge your friends locally on split controls.
  • Dark Night Circuits: Atmospheric racing with glowing headlights and neon streetlights.
  • Custom Supercars: Unlock high-performance sports cars built for midnight speed.
  • Nitro Boost: Engage high-octane nitrous to blast past opponents on straightaways.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Mastering 2 Player Dark Racing requires quick reflexes and precise cornering in low-visibility night tracks.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Player 1 Steer & Drive: W, A, S, D keys.
  • Player 1 Nitro: Shift key.
  • Player 2 Steer & Drive: Arrow Keys (Up, Left, Down, Right).
  • Player 2 Nitro: Spacebar.

Pro Tips

  • Save your Nitro boost for the final straightaway to snatch victory at the finish line!
  • Tap the brakes before entering sharp dark corners to maintain maximum drift traction.
