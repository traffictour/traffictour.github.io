ABOUT BURNIN RUBBER 5 XS

Burnin Rubber 5 XS is an explosive combat racing game combining high-speed vehicle racing with heavy weapons firepower! Mount machine guns, rocket launchers, and laser cannons onto armored muscle cars and blast opponents off the track.

Earn cash reward bonuses by destroying enemy vehicles, executing tactical takedowns, and setting record lap times across intense battle tracks.

Key Features

  • Heavy Weaponry Arsenal: Equip machine guns, homing missiles, and landmines.
  • Armored Combat Vehicles: Unlock heavy battle cars, sports cars, and tanks.
  • High-Speed Destruction: Explosive physics with destructible environment hazards.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Balance your focus between driving line accuracy and aiming your weapons at rival racers.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer & Drive: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys.
  • Primary Weapon Fire: Spacebar or Left Click.
  • Secondary Weapon: Shift or Right Click.
  • Nitro Boost: Z or X key.

Pro Tips

  • Save your homing rockets for when rivals try to overtake you on long straight stretches!
