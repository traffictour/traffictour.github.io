ABOUT BOAT DRIFT

Boat Drift takes racing off dry land and onto high-speed ocean waves! Take control of customized racing speedboats and hydroplanes, carving sharp drift turns around ocean buoys and narrow river bends.

Feel the hydrodynamic water physics as you slide across wave crests, dodge obstacles, and out-maneuver rival boat captains in thrilling nautical tournaments!

Key Features

  • Hydrodynamic Drift Physics: Slide sideways across realistic water currents.
  • Custom Speedboats: Upgrade boat engines, rudders, and hull hydrodynamics.
  • Scenic Aquatic Tracks: Race through tropical lagoons, river canyons, and harbor docks.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Navigating water requires anticipating drift momentum earlier than asphalt racing.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer Boat: A, D or Left / Right Arrow.
  • Throttle / Forward: W or Up Arrow.
  • Reverse / Brake: S or Down Arrow.
  • Drift Rudder Lock: Hold Spacebar while turning.

Pro Tips

  • Release the throttle briefly at turn apexes to let the stern swing outward for longer drift combos.
