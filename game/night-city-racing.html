ABOUT NIGHT CITY RACING

Night City Racing immerses you in a cyberpunk-inspired neon metropolis! Drive high-tech supercars through neon-lit streets, take on high-speed drag races, and launch off giant urban stunt ramps under the night sky.

Equip neon underglow, tune your vehicle's acceleration, and enter 2-player or single-player racing modes to conquer the neon night city!

Key Features

  • Neon Cyber Aesthetics: Glow-in-the-dark city streets and futuristic supercar designs.
  • 3 Thrilling Game Modes: Racing Mode, Drag Race Challenge, and Free Stunt Park.
  • 2-Player Support: Race side-by-side with a friend on split controls.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

In Drag Race mode, shift gears right when the needle hits the green zone to achieve flawless acceleration!

Controls & Keybindings

  • Player 1 Drive: W, A, S, D keys.
  • Player 1 Nitro: Shift key.
  • Player 2 Drive: Arrow Keys.
  • Player 2 Nitro: M or Spacebar.

Pro Tips

  • Use the Free Stunt Park mode to practice continuous drift loops before entering drag tournaments!
