GAME OVERVIEW & EXPLANATION

Welcome to Jelly Truck, where you'll experience a unique driving adventure with jelly-like vehicles! Get ready to navigate through challenging terrain and obstacles in this fun and addictive game.

What We Offer

Jelly Truck offers a refreshing twist on traditional driving games with its squishy and bouncy jelly vehicles. Choose your favorite jelly truck and embark on a journey through colorful and whimsical landscapes.

Gameplay

In Jelly Truck, your goal is to drive your jelly vehicle through various levels while avoiding obstacles and reaching the finish line. The soft and bouncy nature of your truck adds an extra layer of challenge as you navigate through uneven terrain and tricky obstacles.

Controls:

  • Use the arrow keys or WASD to accelerate, brake, and steer your jelly truck.
  • Balance your truck carefully to avoid tipping over and losing control.

Features

  • Dozens of challenging levels with increasing difficulty
  • Unique and colorful graphics with dynamic physics
  • Unlockable jelly truck skins and customization options
  • Compete against your own best times or challenge friends in multiplayer mode
  • Regular updates with new levels and content to keep the game fresh
