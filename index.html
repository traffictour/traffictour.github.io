ABOUT TRAFFIC TOUR - ULTIMATE HIGHWAY RACING

Traffic Tour is a brand new endless arcade racing game that takes you to another level of smooth driving simulations and high-quality graphic quality. Designed for traffic racing fans with advanced features, Traffic Tour offers an exhilarating highway driving experience through real-time traffic, featuring over 40 customizable cars, realistic vehicle cockpits, dynamic weather, and 5 distinct game modes!

In Traffic Tour, your objective is to speed down infinite highway lanes, maneuver through dense traffic of cars and trucks, collect rewards, and upgrade your vehicle arsenal. Whether you want to relax in Free Run mode or compete fiercely against global rivals in real-time multiplayer, Traffic Tour provides unmatched velocity and excitement directly in your web browser.

Key Game Features

  • 40+ Unique Cars: Unlock sports cars, muscle cars, hypercars, and SUVs with realistic engine sounds and customizable paints & rims.
  • 5 Thrilling Game Modes: Multiplayer mode (real-time PvP), Career mode (100+ missions), Time Trial, Endless Highway, and Free Run.
  • 5 Stunning Environments: Highway, City, Desert, Rain, and Snow with dynamic Day & Night cycles.
  • 1st Person Cockpit View: Immersive first-person camera view with functional dashboard meters and realistic steering animation.
  • No Fuel or Time Limits: Pure endless driving with instant restarts and smooth 60 FPS performance.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS GUIDE

Mastering Traffic Tour requires sharp reflexes and strategic lane switching. Follow the controls and expert driving tips below to maximize your high score and earn maximum cash rewards:

Controls & Keybindings

  • Steer Left / Right: Use W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys.
  • Accelerate: Press Up Arrow or W key.
  • Brake / Reverse: Press Down Arrow or S key.
  • Nitrous Boost: Press Shift or Spacebar for extra speed boost.
  • Change Camera View: Press C key to switch between 1st Person interior and 3rd Person camera angles.

Pro Driving Tips for Maximum Score

  • Overtake Closely: Passing vehicles closely at speeds over 100 km/h triggers close-call bonuses and multiplier combos.
  • Drive in Opposite Lane: Driving against traffic flow in Two-Way mode doubles your score accumulation rate!
  • Upgrade Nitro & Engine: Prioritize top speed and nitro acceleration upgrades to conquer harder Career missions.
